# Open Balls

{: .definition }
> Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space.
>
> An <u><strong> open ball </strong></u> of radius $r$ centered at $x_0$ is defined as:
>
> $$
> B_r(x_0) = {x \in X | d(x_0,x) < r}.
> $$
