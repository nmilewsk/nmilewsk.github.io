# <u>Chaper 1: Metric Spaces</u>

Topology can, very broadly, be referred to as the study of open sets. But what exactly IS an open set? And why do we care to study these open sets? Open sets enable us to discuss and analyze a sense of distance between elements. Although this sense of distance will become qualitative down the road as we dive further into topology, it is beneficial and intuitive to first understand how open sets may be defined quantitatively.
